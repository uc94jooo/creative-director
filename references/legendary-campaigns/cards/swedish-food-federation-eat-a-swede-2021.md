# Eat a Swede (Swedish Food Federation · 2021)

## Insight
The world needs to change how it eats before the population hits 10 billion, but giving up favorite foods is one of the world's least favorite truths — so sustainability messaging gets politely ignored. Anchoring theory offered a way in: if you position an alternative so extreme that it is genuinely unpalatable, every other sustainable protein suddenly looks reasonable by comparison. A real Swedish academic (Magnus Söderlund) had already found 8% of people considered eating human meat products acceptable, giving the fiction a disturbing seed of fact.

## Mechanic
McCann Stockholm produced an 18-minute mockumentary following Erik Karlsson, an earnest young scientist determined to launch lab-grown human meat — specifically, Swedes, "the ethical choice" (nature-loving, low-antibiotic, chemical-free), guest-starring Alexander Skarsgård as the prospective donor. The film blurred reality and fiction by casting Prof. Söderlund as the catalyst, then infiltrated the film festival circuit (winning Best Documentary at Paris Play Film Festival) rather than launching as advertising. A premiere in Stockholm anchored a themed day of lectures for food-industry decision-makers, journalists and influencers; bite-size cuts spread through Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and podcasts, all driving to a landing page for the Swedish Food Federation.

## Why it worked
P11 (Absurd as Carrier): cannibalism is the perfect absurdity — shocking enough to guarantee attention, fictional enough to be safe, and logically adjacent to the real argument about lab-grown protein. P16 (Long-form Drama): the mockumentary format earned 18 minutes of engagement and festival legitimacy that a 30-second sustainability spot could never buy. P02 (Enemy or Conflict): the film manufactured a debate, and the debate did the media work — 500M impressions, 100+ features, government ministers drawn in, 98% positive sentiment, +360% Google searches for Swedish food. By moving the needle far in the wrong direction, radical-seeming proteins became acceptable — the anchoring strategy executed as entertainment. Cannes Entertainment Grand Prix 2022.

## Steal
When your category's honest message is a lecture nobody wants ("change how you eat"), don't argue the position — anchor it. Stage an option so extreme that your actual ask becomes the moderate, sensible middle. And if your story is strong enough to be entertainment, distribute it as entertainment first (festivals, culture press) so advertising money only amplifies earned legitimacy.
