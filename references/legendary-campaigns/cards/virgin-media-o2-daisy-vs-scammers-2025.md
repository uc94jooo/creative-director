# Daisy vs Scammers (Virgin Media O2 · 2025)

## Insight
7 in 10 Brits are targeted by scam calls, and every minute a scammer spends on a real, distracted victim is a minute they don't spend defrauding someone. The only defence that scales against an industrial scam operation is wasting the scammers' own time faster than they can waste anyone else's.

## Mechanic
Virgin Media O2 built Daisy: an AI-powered "granny" persona — knits, tells long-winded anecdotes, worries about her cat Fluffy — trained on real scammer call data supplied by UK scambaiter Jim Browning and voiced with custom speech synthesis. Daisy's number was seeded onto scammer call-lists; when scammers rang, Daisy picked up and just kept them talking, some for over 45 minutes, using a bottomless well of confused tangents (including trying to read out her nonexistent "National Cat Insurance Number"). Every call was recorded, and the funniest, most outrageous ones became short films and social content that publicised the tool and normalised talking about scam-baiting.

## Why it worked
P11 (Absurd as Carrier): a rambling AI granny is funny enough to share, but the humour carries a completely serious utility underneath — every laugh is also proof the tool works. P14 (Product as Proof): rather than telling customers "we take fraud seriously," O2 built and deployed a working weapon against scammers and let the call recordings be the evidence. Grounding Daisy's script in real scammer call data (not comedy writing) gave the persona enough tradecraft to actually hold scammers' attention, which is what made the time-wasting real rather than performative. Won 3 Grand Prix at the 2025 Shorty Awards (AI, Generative AI, Technology categories), plus recognition at The One Show and Clio.

## Steal
When your brand's job is to protect people from a bad actor, build something that actively wastes the bad actor's time rather than just warning your customers — the demonstrated cost you impose on the adversary is more convincing than any awareness message, and the byproduct content markets itself.
