# Speaking in Colour (Sherwin-Williams Coil Coatings · 2022)

## Insight
Sherwin-Williams offers architects the world's largest color set — 50,000+ colors — but that abundance is the problem: in a world of endless color, how do you find THE ONE? Color is personal and linguistic before it is visual. "Red" means crimson to one architect and bright apple red to another; people describe the color they want through memories, places and moods, not hex codes. Yet the industry made them page through physical chips and catalogs — an underwhelming process for such an emotional decision.

## Mechanic
Speaking in Colour is a voice-activated web app that lets customers find their perfect color through natural language. Say "crystal clear Caribbean ocean" or "sparkling champagne at sunset" and NLP deconstructs the phrase, runs an image query across millions of online images, and Google Vision AI derives and force-ranks the composite colors by volume to build a personalized palette. A custom algorithm then transliterates subjective refinements — "make it dimmer," "add warmth," "more like the 1980s," "more gray than blue" — into mathematical RGB adjustments, iterating conversationally until the user lands on their one-in-a-million color. As a byproduct, every conversation feeds the largest dataset of color attribution in the world, mapping how culture and geography shape what people mean by words like "moody" or "misty."

## Why it worked
P18 (Tech as Canvas): the AI stack isn't a gimmick bolted onto an ad — it is the product, converting the internet's infinite imagery into an interface between human memory and paint chemistry. P14 (Product as Proof): the tool demonstrates the brand's core claim ("we can create any color imaginable") better than any campaign could, by letting the customer experience it firsthand. It also proved B2B work can be human-centric: the experience was so inspiring it transcended the coil coatings segment and was re-engineered for Sherwin-Williams' consumer retail stores — the rare B2B idea that flowed downstream to B2C.

## Steal
When your category's buying process is functional but the underlying decision is emotional, rebuild the interface around how people naturally talk about the decision, not how your catalog is organized. And instrument it: a tool that helps customers choose is also a machine for harvesting the language of your market.
