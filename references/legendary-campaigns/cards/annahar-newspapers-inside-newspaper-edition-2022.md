# Newspapers Inside The Newspaper Edition (AnNahar Newspaper · 2022)

## Insight
Being a journalist in Lebanon is dangerous: voices critical of the government have been persecuted, imprisoned and assassinated — including AnNahar's own editor-in-chief Gebran Tueni, killed in 2005. Dozens of newspapers with 40-, 60-, 70-year histories have been forced to shut down by political pressure, threats or economic collapse. A silenced newspaper is presumed dead forever. The sharpest possible proof that "the press will never be silenced" is not a statement — it's a resurrection.

## Mechanic
On December 12th, the anniversary of Tueni's assassination, AnNahar released a daily edition that was not one newspaper but seven. Inside its front and back covers, spread after spread revealed the front pages of previously extinct Lebanese newspapers, brought back to life — written by the very same journalists who once ran them, free to write without fear. Each spread was designed true to the defunct paper's original identity, down to column widths and mastheads, spanning rival political sides and Lebanon's different languages. The edition ran on newsstands nationwide, plus online and social.

## Why it worked
P01 (Format as Idea): the medium *is* the message — a newspaper physically hosting its dead competitors turns the product itself into the proof of free press. P04 (Brand as Activist): AnNahar didn't advocate for silenced journalists; it handed them back their pages. P13 (Truth Telling): reviving censored voices verbatim, under their original mastheads, is unfalsifiable defiance — the words themselves are the evidence. Anchoring the act to the assassination anniversary gave it moral weight beyond PR. The edition sold out, became one of the paper's highest-selling issues, earned $13M+ in media impressions, and drew public commitments from members of parliament. Print & Publishing Grand Prix, Cannes Lions 2023.

## Steal
When your category's casualties are visible (shut-down shops, discontinued products, silenced voices), your platform can host their revival — solidarity with fallen rivals reads as leadership, not charity. And date your act: tying the release to a martyr's anniversary converts a stunt into a commemoration the culture is obliged to respect.
