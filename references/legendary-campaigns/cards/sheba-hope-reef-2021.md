# Hope Reef (Sheba · 2021)

## Insight
Fish is the main protein in Sheba's best-selling products, and data showed the ocean could be empty of fish by 2048 — with 25% of all marine life dependent on coral reefs. But the deeper communications insight was emotional: images of destruction and despair make the climate crisis feel insurmountable, so people disengage. Sheba had something almost no one in the climate conversation had — powerful proof that man-made solutions to man-made problems work. Good news was the gift. Coral today means fish tomorrow.

## Mechanic
In the coral triangle off Indonesia, Sheba used its Reef Star system to regrow a barren site of dead coral into a thriving reef the size of two Olympic swimming pools — planted so that, viewed from the air, it spells HOPE. The world's first billboard made of living coral was then made visible to billions: granted landmark status on Google Earth, Maps and Street View, over 1 million people explored it. A YouTube fundraiser turned Sheba into a content creator whose ad revenue from views funded a crowdfunded reef, and the campaign anchored the world's largest coral restoration program — a commitment to restore 185,000+ m2 of reef by 2029, supported by the UN and WWF. Two years on: 99% dead to 70% coral coverage, +300% fish abundance, +175% average fish size, and new builds underway in five more countries. The regrown coral is growing so well it will eventually swallow the billboard itself.

## Why it worked
P04 (Brand as Activist): the purpose claim is inseparable from the supply chain — a fish-based brand securing the future of fish is self-interest and altruism in the same act, which makes it credible. P17 (Design as Idea): spelling HOPE in living coral compresses the entire strategy into one aerial image; the medium (regrown reef) is the proof of the message. P18 (Tech as Canvas): Google Earth turned a remote reef into globally accessible out-of-home, and YouTube's ad-revenue mechanics turned passive viewing into funding. Commercially it proved purpose can defend a price premium: 2.5 billion earned impressions, brand awareness +17%, purchase intent +14%, and 308% campaign ROI in activating markets while copycats discounted.

## Steal
When your category is racing to the bottom on product parity, look upstream in your supply chain for a threatened resource your brand visibly depends on — then fix it in a way that photographs. And design the proof to outgrow the ad: the best purpose executions are ones where success eventually erases the branding.
