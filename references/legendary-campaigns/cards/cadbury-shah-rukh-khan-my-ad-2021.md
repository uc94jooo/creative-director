# Shah Rukh Khan-My-Ad (Cadbury · 2021)

## Insight
Diwali is Cadbury Celebrations' biggest business moment, but post-pandemic India in 2021 was abandoning the small local stores that had been consumers' lifeline — hygiene caution, doorstep-delivery habits, and e-commerce giants' festive sales had pushed neighborhood shops to the bottom of people's minds. Big brands have big money to hire famous faces; the small guys — the ones still hurting — never could. What if the biggest movie star in the world became their brand ambassador too?

## Mechanic
Using machine learning (Rephrase.AI), Cadbury regenerated Shah Rukh Khan's face and voice to speak the name of a different local store in every ad. Pin-code data mapped stores in the most impacted categories and cities across four retail types (fashion, footwear, general, electronics) in Cadbury's distribution network; the AI system generated thousands of localized permutations of the same ad, served on Facebook and YouTube featuring the stores physically closest to each viewer in real time. Then the power went to the people: a platform let any small business owner create their own version of Shah Rukh Khan promoting their shop, shareable via WhatsApp and social pages. 130,000 versions of the ad were created by small retailers across the country in a two-week Diwali window.

## Why it worked
P18 (Tech as Canvas): the first hyper-personalized ad at this scale — AI didn't decorate the idea, it *was* the mechanism that made one celebrity infinitely divisible. P09 (User as Co-Author): store owners generating their own SRK ads turned the campaign into 130,000 self-distributing campaigns, each shared with pride through the owner's own network. P07 (Invisible Brand): the ads sold the stores, not the chocolate — Cadbury's generosity message ("this Diwali, buy local") was enacted rather than stated, which is exactly why it earned love. 1.4B+ impressions, 94M+ views, 33 million Celebrations packs sold (the brand's highest ever), 0.44 million stores selling, 30% Diwali growth. Titanium, Cannes Lions 2022; Creative Effectiveness Grand Prix, Cannes Lions 2023.

## Steal
Take your brand's most expensive exclusive asset — a superstar, a jingle, prime media — and use technology to share it with those who could never afford it; generosity at scale buys more equity than exclusivity ever will. And when personalizing with AI, hand the generation tool to the beneficiaries: co-authored ads travel through networks paid media can't reach.
