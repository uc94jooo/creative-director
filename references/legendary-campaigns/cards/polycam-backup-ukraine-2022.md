# Backup Ukraine (Polycam x UNESCO · 2022)

## Insight
At the core of Russia's war propaganda is the claim that Ukraine has no unique, sovereign national identity — and according to Blue Shield, destroying a country's cultural heritage is the fastest way to erase that identity. With 191 cultural sites destroyed in the first month of the war, physical protection (sandbags, covers) was never going to be enough. Preserving heritage wasn't about helping museums; it was about a people's right to exist. If destruction is nearly inevitable, the only guaranteed protection is a backup — in the cloud, where no bombs can reach.

## Mechanic
Polycam customized its photogrammetry algorithm and, with UNESCO and Blue Shield Denmark, released a free app to every iOS and Android user in Ukraine. Any citizen could walk around a monument with a phone, press record, and produce a museum-grade high-definition 3D model in minutes — quality previously reserved for film studios — automatically saved with GPS data to a secure open cloud archive. The system was built with archival experts: data held to museum standards, cold storage guaranteed for five years after the war, export to 10+ 3D formats, and privacy measures letting museums mask collection locations against looting and targeted strikes. The Ministry of Culture in Kyiv issued official endorsements so volunteers could scan without suspicion of espionage; the Heritage Emergency Rescue Initiative coordinated volunteers and onboarded local museums.

## Why it worked
P18 (Tech as Canvas): consumer photogrammetry was repurposed as a national defense tool for identity — the technology is the idea. P09 (User as Co-Author): with local authorities stretched to the limit, the only way to scale in wartime was to deputize the entire population; every citizen became an archivist. P04 (Brand as Activist): a 3D-scanning startup took a stand at infrastructure level, not message level. Urgency did the media work: 170+ articles in 10 days, 135 civilian volunteers, 6 NGOs, and a scanning pace (44% weekly growth) putting all 26,800 monuments on track — with UNESCO's National Commissioner publicly urging neighbouring countries to start scanning too. It won the Digital Craft Grand Prix at Cannes 2022.

## Steal
When a threat is unstoppable, shift the brief from prevention to preservation — you can't stop the bomb, but you can make destruction meaningless. Ask what professional-grade capability your product can hand to ordinary people in a crisis, then remove every barrier (free, phone-based, minutes not hours). Partnering with the institutions who own legitimacy (UNESCO, ministries) converts a tech demo into national infrastructure.
