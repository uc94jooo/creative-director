# My Japan Railway (JR Group · 2022)

## Insight
Japan Railway's trains are so integral to daily life that 150 years of service made them invisible — people took the network for granted, and only 6% of Japanese had visited all 47 prefectures. The romance of rail travel hadn't disappeared; it had been buried under routine. The way back wasn't to advertise the trains, but to give every station a personal identity worth collecting — turning access points into destinations of personal significance.

## Mechanic
For JR's 150th anniversary, Dentsu researched the history of stations nationwide — historical records, newspaper archives, photos, social media memories — and created woodcut-style stamps for some 600 stations, each in the signature colours of the six JR companies. A web app lets travellers collect a station's stamp on their smartphone when passing through. The digital stamps keep an analogue soul: pressing the screen longer varies the "stamping force," personalising each impression. Metadata shows how many others collected the same stamp, creating shared experience. Stamps can be arranged by collection order, rarity, popularity, or as a personalised map of one's travels — each user's own "My Japan Railway." In-station billboards displayed all stamps arranged into a map of Japan; stamp books, posters, and promotional items rewarded in-app tasks.

## Why it worked
P17 (Design as Idea): the craft is the campaign — 600 bespoke woodcut stamps turned corporate infrastructure into a collectible design system, winning the Industry Craft Grand Prix at Cannes 2023. P10 (Serialization & Ritual): stamp collecting converted commuting into a game with no endpoint — 770,000 users collected over 2,000,000 stamps, and everyday journeys became progress toward completion. P09 (User as Co-Author): the ownable output is each person's unique map — the brand built the system, travellers built their own meaning inside it. Results: 71% said train travel became more fun, 68% wanted to visit new places, 16,000+ social mentions, 18M+ impressions, with regional economies benefiting from revived domestic travel.

## Steal
When a brand is infrastructure — used daily, noticed never — don't advertise its importance; fragment it into collectible pieces with individual identities. Completion mechanics turn habitual usage into exploration, and a personal collection makes a public utility feel like private property.
