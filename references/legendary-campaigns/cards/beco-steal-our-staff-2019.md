# Steal Our Staff (BECO · 2019)

## Insight
1.1 million disabled people are out of work in the UK; while 80% of the general population is employed, the figure for disabled people is just 48% — and though 90% of companies prioritize diversity, only 4% consider disability. BECo, a 166-year-old social enterprise soap brand where 80% of staff have disabilities, realized its growth logic contained a beautiful inversion: if its people get hired away to bigger jobs, BECo can create new vacancies for disabled people looking for their first step onto the career ladder.

## Mechanic
BECo flipped the recruitment campaign on its head and invited Britain's employers to #StealOurStaff. The soap packaging on Waitrose and Sainsbury's shelves was redesigned to feature employees' actual CVs — mini billboards reaching 1.7m+ shoppers — while the website carried fuller staff profiles plus practical advice on hiring people with disabilities. An open letter in the Guardian urged businesses to act; outdoor posters subverted famous brand endlines; social films starring BECo employees riffed on TV's audio-description function (Sainsbury's donated a 60" spot for International Day of Persons with Disabilities); bespoke product boxes went to business leaders, and social activity targeted specific employers by name.

## Why it worked
P03 (Behavior Inversion): companies guard their talent — a brand begging you to poach its people is a pattern-break that earns attention and instantly communicates confidence in those people's abilities. P14 (Product as Proof): the packaging itself carries the CVs, so every unit sold doubles as a job application in front of a mass audience, at zero media cost. P04 (Brand as Activist): the campaign attacks the disability employment gap structurally — not by asking for sympathy but by showcasing capability and handing employers the how-to. Zero media spend reached 10.1% of UK adults (£119,713 equivalent media value); brand awareness grew 83%, rate of sale +96% YoY, distribution up from 50 to 250 stores; 40+ employers made contact and employee Allan received a job offer. Grand Prix, Cannes Lions 2020/2021.

## Steal
Ask what your brand would do if it truly wanted its social problem solved — even at apparent cost to itself. Sacrificial generosity ("steal our staff") is the credibility proof that no purpose statement can buy. And for challenger budgets: your packaging is the media channel you already own at shelf-level scale.
