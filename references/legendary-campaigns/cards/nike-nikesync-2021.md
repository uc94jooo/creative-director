# NikeSync (Nike · 2021)

## Insight
27,000 women train daily with the Nike Training Club app — using sports science built around male physiology. But women are not just small men: the menstrual cycle influences energy, recovery, and metabolism throughout the month. Sport and periods are rarely discussed together, education on female physiology is missing from school curricula, and women wrongly assume they need to rest on bleed days. Her period is a training barrier only because of a knowledge gap — if she understood the hormones at play, she could train in sync with them, prevent injury, and reach new limits.

## Mechanic
Working with female athlete performance expert Dr. Stacy Sims, Nike broke the menstrual cycle into three defined phases and launched NikeSync on the Nike Training Club app in February 2021: a collection of workouts tailored to the hormonal profile of each phase, with a strong digital identity to carry the science. Members were driven to the collection through @nikewomen social content, email, in-app editorial, a Menstrual Cycle Audio Guided Run fronted by Paula Radcliffe, and a launch on the Gurls Talk podcast. Year-round educational editorial — from how the cycle affects stress, immunity, and cravings to a taboo-busting "Never Asked Questions" series — completed the ecosystem. Results: +60% push notification engagement vs. average, +199% weekly retained female members vs. the prior period, and the highest follows and clicks for @nikewomen since August 2020.

## Why it worked
P13 (Truth Telling): the campaign named an uncomfortable industry truth — all mainstream training methodology is male-default — and Nike gained first-mover authority simply by saying it. P14 (Product as Proof): the message wasn't an empowerment film but a functioning training program; the product itself does the persuading. P03 (Behavior Inversion): it flips the assumed behavior ("rest during your period") into its opposite ("train with your cycle, every day of the month"), which is inherently newsworthy to the audience living the problem. Proactive utility inside an app women already used daily beat any media spend for credibility.

## Steal
Audit your category's default settings: whose body, schedule, or context was the "standard" model built for? Serving the excluded majority with a product fix — not an ad about them — converts a taboo into a retention engine. The knowledge your audience was never taught is content your brand can own.
