# Transition Body Lotion (Vaseline · 2023)

## Insight
Vaseline's mission is "Healthy Skin for All," but there wasn't a single skincare product catered to transgender women and the specific skin issues — dryness, dullness, hyperpigmentation — caused by hormone therapy during gender-affirming transition. Trans women were improvising with trial and error across men's and women's products. The agency answered a communication brief with a product idea instead: as the case film puts it, "It's not every day that the agency responds with a product idea in a communication briefing. But when that product idea fits perfectly with your brand purpose, you just have to do it."

## Mechanic
Two years of co-creation with the transgender community — focus groups, gender-reassignment specialists, dermatologists, and ongoing feedback on formula and fragrance — produced the world's first clinically proven skincare product for transgender women, under Vaseline's premium Pro Derma line. The hero ingredient is Isoflavones, a plant-based oestrogen never before used in the category, which supports skin through hormonal change. Launched in Thailand on International Transgender Day of Visibility with TVCs, online film, print, and social speaking directly to the community, and stocked permanently in all 670 Watsons stores. Results: 158 million impressions in Thailand, 100% positive sentiment, purchase intent 725% above benchmark, sell-outs in many stores, and international expansion plus a trans-men's product now in development.

## Why it worked
P14 (Product as Proof): instead of a rainbow-flag campaign, the brand made a permanent SKU — inclusion you can buy on a shelf, with patented R&D behind it. As one voice in the film says, it's not "throwing a rainbow around something"; the politics arrives "in the form of beauty." P04 (Brand as Activist): Vaseline took a stance simply by treating an ignored community as a legitimate consumer segment with clinical needs — dignifying rather than tokenizing, because the community shaped every step from formulation to launch messaging. Launch timing (Day of Visibility) and permanent retail presence turned a campaign moment into category infrastructure.

## Steal
The strongest purpose work answers a comms brief with a product brief. Find the audience your category structurally ignores, quantify their unmet functional need, and build the SKU with them — a permanent shelf placement says more than any manifesto film, and the community's ownership of the process is what makes them your media.
