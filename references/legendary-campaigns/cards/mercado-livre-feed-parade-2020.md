# Feed Parade (Mercado Livre · 2020)

## Insight
São Paulo's Paulista Avenue hosts the world's biggest Pride Parade — a right the Brazilian LGBTQIA+ community won through decades of struggle. In 2020, amid a wave of violent anti-LGBTQIA+ attacks, the pandemic cancelled the parade — taking away not just an event but the community's hard-won public act of visibility. For Mercado Livre, the parade's traditional sponsor and Latin America's biggest e-commerce, the question was whether it could deliver something more than products in lockdown: a place for the community to show up and be counted.

## Mechanic
A drone photographed all 2.5 km of Paulista Avenue, cropped into nearly 300 photos posted in precise order to a new Instagram profile, @FeedParade, reconstructing the entire avenue as a scrollable feed. On June 14 — the day the real parade would have happened — Gloria Groove (Brazil's biggest LGBTQIA+ artist) and fifteen influencers across the community's spectrum invited people to comment and get tagged at the exact spot on the avenue where they usually march. The feed filled in 15 minutes at 50 comments per second. Two weeks later, Gloria Groove released a music video featuring the Instagram handle of every one of the 60,000+ people tagged. 500M impressions, 98% positive mentions, 1200% above-average engagement.

## Why it worked
P01 (Format as Idea): the Instagram grid itself becomes the avenue — the platform's native format is the medium, the map, and the monument at once. P09 (User as Co-Author): a comment equals attendance; the campaign is literally made of its participants, and the music video eternalizes each name individually rather than as a crowd statistic. P06 (Limitation as Power): lockdown, the thing that cancelled the parade, is what forced the invention of a parade no one could cancel. The location specificity — tag yourself where you usually stand — turned a digital gesture into an act of memory and presence.

## Steal
When a physical ritual is blocked, don't stream a substitute — rebuild the ritual's geography inside a native digital format and let people occupy their own spot in it. Participation counts double when it's individually addressable: name every participant, don't aggregate them.
