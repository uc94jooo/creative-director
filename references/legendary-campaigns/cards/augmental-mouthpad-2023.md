# MouthPad^ (Augmental · 2023)

## Insight
For people with quadriplegia or upper-limb impairment, existing assistive tech is frustratingly limited — one user describes losing his front teeth from years of smashing his face on a quad stick, another dropping out of school because voice control was unusable. Meanwhile the tongue, "the eleventh finger," has the precision and dexterity of a fingertip — and its potential for controlling digital devices had never been used.

## Mechanic
MouthPad^ is a smart retainer that rests on the roof of the mouth, letting the tongue operate like a finger on a trackpad. Custom-fit via intraoral 3D scanning and biocompatible resin, it encapsulates a flexible circuit board with sensors and a machine-learning processor, transmitting via Bluetooth to any device. Eight dimensions of input — tongue, jaw, head, and sip-puff gestures — control cursor, clicks and gestures hands-free. Developed with the disability community under universal design principles, with wireless software updates. The launch campaign let users demonstrate it in their own words, on no media budget.

## Why it worked
P14 (Product as Proof): there is no advertising claim here — the invention *is* the message, and real users' reactions ("it'll almost be like getting my right arm back") are the film. P18 (Tech as Canvas): a genuine technological breakthrough generates news value that money can't buy — 1.3B media impressions and a 5775% surge in website traffic with zero spend. P06 (Limitation as Power): the campaign reframes disability from deficit to capability by celebrating the tongue's untapped dexterity, positioning users as early adopters of a device "eventually everyone will be using." 1,000+ pre-orders in the first two days. Innovation Grand Prix, Cannes Lions 2023.

## Steal
When the client's product is a real breakthrough, get out of its way: cast actual users, let unpolished testimony carry the emotion, and spend on earned news rather than paid media. And look for the "underused organ" insight — capability that everyone possesses but no interface has claimed is a whitespace both for products and for stories.
