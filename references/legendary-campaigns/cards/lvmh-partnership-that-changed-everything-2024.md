# The Partnership That Changed Everything (LVMH · 2024)

## Insight
The Olympic Games are the most watched sports competition on earth — and the most ad-regulated. Olympic rules ban advertising in competition areas, making sponsors invisible in the broadcasts they paid to be part of. For LVMH, a Premium Partner of Paris 2024, being a logo on screen was both impossible and beneath the house. So instead of buying visibility, the Group claimed a new role: co-creator of the Games' most iconic moments, expressing its mission — the art of crafting dreams — through the ceremonies themselves.

## Mechanic
LVMH introduced a new co-producer status and put its Maisons to work inside the Games. Dior created haute couture costumes for ceremony performers. Berluti tailored suits for every Team France athlete. Chaumet crafted the medals like jewels, setting fragments of original Eiffel Tower iron at their centre. Louis Vuitton reinterpreted its iconic trunks to hold the medals, integrated into a dedicated broadcast sequence, and delivered them to every athlete on a signature tray at each victory ceremony. Craftsmanship wasn't shown alongside the event — it was woven into every ceremony, every medal, every victory the world watched.

## Why it worked
P06 (Limitation as Power): the broadcast ad ban became the brief — barred from advertising, LVMH became un-cut-out-able, because you cannot edit the sponsor out of the medal itself. P08 (Craft as Message): each Maison communicated through its actual métier — tailoring, jewellery, trunk-making — so the "ad" was the savoir-faire, indistinguishable from the product. P05 (Cultural Hijack): the Games' emotional peaks (victory ceremonies, the medals, the host nation's pride) carried the brand into a global audience with total authenticity, generating €350M in earned media and the Luxury & Lifestyle Grand Prix at Cannes 2025 — widely described as changing sponsorship forever.

## Steal
When the platform bans advertising, stop trying to advertise and start manufacturing the platform's own sacred objects. Ask: which artefact of this event carries the most emotion, and could our craft literally make it? Embedded contribution survives every regulation that kills a logo.
