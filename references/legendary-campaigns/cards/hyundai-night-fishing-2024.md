# Night Fishing (Hyundai Motor Company · 2024)

## Insight
IONIQ is Hyundai's flagship EV brand, but despite massive ad budgets its recognition among the MZ generation was just 1.4%; 80% of buyers were middle-aged and only 3% in their twenties. Worse, electric-taxi subsidies had branded IONIQ as a "taxi brand" in young minds. This audience voluntarily consumes entertainment and reflexively skips advertising — so the only way in was to stop making ads and start making something they would choose to watch, and even pay for.

## Mechanic
「Night Fishing」 is a 13-minute suspense film shot entirely through the seven cameras installed in the Hyundai IONIQ — the car never appears as product placement because the car *is* the camera and the storyteller. No logos, no full vehicle shots. The film obeyed cinema's rules, not advertising's: world premiere at Sundance's Chef Dance program, the same rating-classification process as feature films, nationwide theatrical release with tickets priced at $1 and a runtime tuned to short-form attention spans. The rollout mirrored a real film release — premiere events, limited merch, behind-the-scenes exhibitions at Hyundai Motor Studios, then OTT and in-flight entertainment. With zero media budget it ranked #1 in audience occupancy (beating Inside Out 2), tripled average ticket sales, extended screenings by 250%, topped OTT rankings for two weeks, and became the first branded content carried by Korean Air. For IONIQ: +200% Gen Z searches, +126% dealership visits, +155% test drives, 13.7B PR value, 767% higher impact than previous campaigns.

## Why it worked
P07 (Invisible Brand): by removing every logo and product shot, the film earned the one thing advertising can't buy from MZ audiences — voluntary attention. Content fandom ignited brand fandom, not the reverse. P16 (Long-form Drama): committing to genuine cinema (festival premiere, classification, box office) gave the work cultural legitimacy that no "branded content" label allows. P06 (Limitation as Power): the car's fixed built-in cameras — an apparent production constraint — became the film's entire formal language, and simultaneously the product demo: every frame is literally proof of what the IONIQ's cameras see. The $1 ticket was the masterstroke of framing: paying anything converts a viewer into an audience member who chose this.

## Steal
If your target skips ads, compete in their entertainment feed under entertainment's rules — festival, rating, box office, reviews — and hide the brand so thoroughly that discovering it becomes the story. Look for the product feature that can be the medium itself, not the subject.
