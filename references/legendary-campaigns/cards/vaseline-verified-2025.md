# Vaseline Verified (Vaseline · 2025)

## Insight
Over 6,000 organic social posts showcase hacks for Vaseline Jelly — beard care, cuticle protection, spice-proofing lips, the Hollywood soft-focus lens trick — while TikTok simultaneously spreads genuinely unsafe advice (one creator films himself eating it). In an era of health misinformation, the brand's biggest asset and biggest risk were the same thing: an uncontrolled folk encyclopedia of product uses. Dismissing it would insult the community; ignoring it would let dangerous trends ride the brand name.

## Mechanic
For the first time, Vaseline's scientists took viral hacks into the lab and rigorously tested them in playful, lab-style videos. Hacks that worked earned an official "Vaseline Verified" seal — the original creators got their years-old videos elevated into brand films and received physical trophy boxes. Dangerous or false hacks were publicly unverified and debunked. Verified creators then leaned into commerce ("I'm officially a Vaseline dealer"), turning the seal into both content engine and social proof. The community kept generating; the lab kept adjudicating.

## Why it worked
P09 (User as Co-Author): the audience had already written the campaign — Vaseline just built the validation layer on top, which respects rather than replaces creator culture. P14 (Product as Proof): every verification is a product demo with scientific rigor attached; the "Wonder Jelly" claim is demonstrated hack by hack instead of asserted. P13 (Truth Telling): debunking its own community's myths bought the brand the credibility to celebrate the real ones, positioning Vaseline as the honest referee in a misinformation era. Being "verified" also flips influencer status dynamics — creators sought the brand's seal, not the other way around.

## Steal
When your product already has an organic folk culture of uses and hacks, don't compete with it — become its certification body. Test publicly, reject some things visibly (the rejections are what make the approvals believable), and reward the original creators so the community amplifies its own validation.
