# Preserved Promos (Ziploc · 2025)

## Insight
Ziploc's entire reason for being is preserving what matters — keeping food fresh and helping households stretch their budgets. But food isn't the only thing in a shopper's life that expires: food coupons do too, and when they lapse, budget-conscious consumers lose real savings and feel the small sting of a missed opportunity. That everyday frustration sat exactly on top of the brand's core benefit. If Ziploc keeps food from going to waste, it could keep savings from going to waste too.

## Mechanic
Shoppers upload a photo or screenshot of any expired food promo and receive an extended offer on their next food purchase — with one catch: to unlock the revived deal, Ziploc products must be in the cart. Built mobile-first as a seamless commerce experience, the system automatically validates the uploaded coupon and applies the discount at checkout once Ziploc is added. Expired promotions from any food brand become renewed value, retailers gain incremental baskets, and every redemption physically pairs the brand with the act of preservation it stands for.

## Why it worked
P14 (Product as Proof): the mechanic is the brand benefit performed literally — Ziploc "preserves" coupons the way it preserves food, so the commerce gimmick doubles as a product demonstration no ad copy could match. P03 (Behavior Inversion): it flips the fixed rule of couponing — expiry is final — into the brand's exclusive gift to give back, converting a universal moment of consumer disappointment into a Ziploc-owned moment of rescue. The conditional unlock (Ziploc must be in the cart) turns goodwill directly into attach-rate: the brand pays for the extended discount only when it wins the sale, making this rare purpose-adjacent creativity with a self-funding commerce engine.

## Steal
List the things in your customer's life that expire, run out, break or get wasted — beyond your product category — and ask which of them your core benefit could metaphorically rescue. Then gate the rescue behind a purchase trigger so the emotional win and the commercial win happen in the same transaction.
