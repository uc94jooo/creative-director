# Clash from the Past (Clash of Clans · 2022)

## Insight
Gamers hate ads but crave lore. After ten years and 300 million daily players, Clash of Clans was a giant — but mobile games never get the cultural credit of Mario, Donkey Kong or Zelda, because those franchises have decades of history and mythology. Fans don't just love a game; they love the game's universe. Clash didn't have that kind of story. So instead of celebrating a 10th anniversary, invent the missing thirty years.

## Mechanic
For the fake 40th anniversary, Wieden+Kennedy built an alternate universe in which Clash had existed since 1982, engineered to trigger a genuine Mandela Effect. The centerpiece: a meticulously fabricated 20-minute documentary shot on five era-specific cameras, with fake commercials, fake morning-show interviews, and scenes from a fake movie. Three era-specific games were built for real — an 80s arcade platformer, a 90s racer, a 2000s open-world RPG — and released inside the Clash app to all 300 million daily players. The story deepened across channels: a Reddit AMA with the fictional founder, an IMDB page, social feeds turned into memory lanes of fake vintage ads, plus real merchandise with Champion, Topps (Garbage Pail Kids cards), and a General Mills cereal sold in actual stores.

## Why it worked
P16 (Long-form Drama): a 20-minute documentary as the hero asset respected the audience's appetite for deep content instead of interrupting it. P08 (Craft as Message): the obsessive period-accurate craft — real cameras, real playable games, real cereal — is what converted a joke into a believable alternate history; the craft was the persuasion. P05 (Cultural Hijack): it borrowed the entire iconography of retro gaming nostalgia to retroactively install Clash into a canon it never belonged to. With zero paid media it became YouTube's #1 trending video (65M+ views), the most upvoted thread ever on Clash's Reddit, the cereal sold out, cards resold for $2,000, and brand sentiment hit an all-time high. Cannes Entertainment Grand Prix 2023.

## Steal
If your brand lacks heritage, fabricate it with total commitment — but make the fiction generous: give fans real playable artifacts, not just claims. The line between joke and lore is production quality; every era-accurate detail buys belief. Anniversaries are boring when they count real years; count imaginary ones and you get a story.
