# Tree Correspondents (Hyundai · 2025)

## Insight
A tree-planting counter (Hyundai's IONIQ Forest hit 1 million trees at its 10-year mark) proves scale but says nothing about whether any single tree is actually thriving — and nobody reads a sustainability report as if a forest could talk back.

## Mechanic
Hyundai installed sensor trackers on IONIQ Forest trees across Brazil, the Czech Republic and Korea, streaming real-time ecological data (growth, soil, weather) alongside external datasets from partners like The Weather Company. A bespoke large language model translated that raw sensor data into first-person narrative posts — the trees became "correspondents," taking over Hyundai's Threads account to report on their own health, surroundings and small daily events, in the voice of the tree itself rather than a brand.

## Why it worked
P07 (Invisible Brand): Hyundai doesn't narrate its own environmental commitment — it hands the microphone to the actual trees the initiative funded, so the proof is literally speaking for itself. P17 (Design as Idea): converting sensor telemetry into first-person voice is a genuine design invention, not a metaphor bolted onto existing data — the LLM's summarization and tone-setting *is* the creative craft, which is why it won across Digital Craft categories (2 Gold Lions plus 1 Silver Lion at Cannes 2025) rather than as a stunt or PR play.

## Steal
If your brand already collects real operational data behind a sustainability or CSR claim, don't just report the aggregate number — give the underlying data a voice and a format (social account, first-person narration) that makes ongoing verification feel like following a person, not auditing a spreadsheet.
