# Today at Apple (Apple · 2017)

## Insight
Apple's retail chief Angela Ahrendts reframed the entire estate with one metaphor: the store is the largest product Apple produces. The architecture is the hardware — so what is the software? A store that only sells transactions runs empty software. People don't return weekly to buy; they return to learn, make, and meet. The reframe converted 495 retail boxes into an ongoing program problem: what runs on this hardware every day?

## Mechanic
Today at Apple installed free, daily creative programming in every Apple Store worldwide: Photo Walks, Kids Labs, Music Hours, coding classes, sketching sessions, poetry sharing. The Genius concept was extended with "Creative Pros" — staff specialized in music and photography rather than repair. The store's design language (Foster + Partners architecture, forum screens, town-square layout) was rebuilt to host sessions, not just display product. The stated ambition was explicitly civic: the store as a town square where "the best of Apple comes together, everybody's invited, and it becomes almost a community hub" — measured not in units sold but in "did you learn something, were you inspired, did you connect with people in the community?"

## Why it worked
P10 (Serialization & Ritual): "Today at" is a promise of daily recurrence — programming, like software, ships continuously, giving customers a reason to return that no product cycle can provide. P17 (Design as Idea): the hardware/software metaphor is itself the creative idea; it dictated everything from staffing to architecture, which is why it topped all 71 metals in Brand Experience & Activation at Cannes 2018. It also inverts retail economics: the most valuable square footage in commerce was given over to activities with no direct transaction — a confidence signal only a brand-as-institution can send.

## Steal
Name your owned asset as a product and ask what its "software" is — the recurring, living layer that changes daily on static infrastructure. A channel people visit out of ritual beats a channel people visit out of need. If your space, app, or page only works when someone intends to buy, it's hardware running nothing.
