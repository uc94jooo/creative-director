# Less Talk, More Bitcoin (Coinbase · 2022)

## Insight
Nearly two-thirds of Americans didn't understand cryptocurrency and didn't think crypto was for "people like them" — but 45% of the growth audience said they'd consider it if someone guided them through the process. The best way to learn is to do. So instead of explaining crypto on advertising's biggest stage, make trying it the ad: no dancing puppies, no talking babies, no celebrities — just an invitation to act.

## Mechanic
Coinbase bought sixty seconds of the 2022 Super Bowl (112M viewers, reaching 70% of its crypto-beginner bullseye) and aired a lo-fi spot of nothing but a QR code bouncing wall to wall in the style of the classic DVD screensaver — an internet icon with a cult following of its own. Scanning led to a simple landing page: $15 in free Bitcoin for new users, a $1M Bitcoin sweepstakes for existing ones. No reruns, no social support, minimal branding — the team worked with NBC and the NFL to push what was legally and technically allowed to air. Results: 20M+ instant landing page hits, 1.4M existing users opted in, 14B+ earned impressions, an estimated $130M earned media value, +300% brand consideration, and 445K+ attributed new sign-ups despite a multi-step ID/SSN onboarding — ROI positive within weeks.

## Why it worked
P01 (Format as Idea): the ad's format — a screensaver, the universal symbol of "nothing happening" — was the entire creative; the anti-spectacle became the spectacle amid $7M-a-slot maximalism. P03 (Behavior Inversion): while every Super Bowl ad performed at a passive audience, this one gave viewers 60 seconds of a task, converting the world's biggest lean-back moment into a lean-forward one. The nostalgia of the bouncing DVD logo made an unbranded QR code feel like a shared in-joke rather than a phishing risk, and the direct mechanic matched the strategic insight exactly: less talk, more doing. Cannes Direct Grand Prix 2022.

## Steal
On a stage where everyone maximizes production, minimizing it is the differentiation — but only if the void is filled with an action. Pair an ultra-low-fi format hijack with a friction-free first step and an immediate incentive. Ask of any awareness buy: what could the audience do during these seconds, not after them?
