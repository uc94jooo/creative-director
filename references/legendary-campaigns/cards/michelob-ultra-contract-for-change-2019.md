# Contract for Change (Michelob Ultra · 2019)

## Insight
Michelob ULTRA Pure Gold, America's first mass organic beer, was an instant success — and then hit a wall: barely 1% of US farmland is organic. Most farmers would love to grow natural food again, but the three-year organic transition means significant investment, lost income while yields drop, and no guaranteed buyer at the end of the road. Farmers weren't refusing to change; they were structurally trapped on a treadmill. AB InBev, the world's largest brewer, had enough purchasing power to remove every one of those barriers with a signature.

## Mechanic
Contract for Change is a legally binding agreement between Anheuser-Busch and American farmers that dismantles the three biggest barriers to going organic: (1) farmers who sign are guaranteed Michelob ULTRA Pure Gold as their first organic customer once the three-year transition completes; (2) during the transition, AB buys their crops for its non-organic beers at a 25% premium, so income never drops; (3) the contract includes in-depth organic-farming training through trusted bodies like the Organic Trade Association and the USDA. After a 2019 pilot, it was offered to all American farmers via hyper-targeted local media, mailed personalized contracts, a phone line and website — with PR carrying it into Forbes, Business Insider and agricultural press. Result: 175 farmers signed and 104,000 acres — 4% of US barley farmland — began transitioning.

## Why it worked
P14 (Product as Proof): the "campaign" is a supply-chain instrument that actually solves the problem it talks about — the communication is just the paperwork made public. P04 (Brand as Activist): instead of a purpose film about sustainable farming, the brand re-engineered its own procurement economics, which no competitor could dismiss as talk. It won the PR Grand Prix at Cannes 2021 and the Creative Effectiveness Grand Prix in 2022 — the rare double that certifies both the story and the business result. The model is openly replicable by any food and beverage company, which makes the brand the author of an industry standard.

## Steal
Find the structural barrier your audience faces — not the attitudinal one — and ask what your company's scale (purchasing power, distribution, legal muscle) could contractually remove. A guarantee is more persuasive than a message; the strongest purpose work is a business term sheet the public gets to read.
