# Tracking Bad Bunny (Rimas Music · 2025)

## Insight
Bad Bunny's power as an artist is inseparable from Puerto Rico itself — he is an icon precisely because he is rooted in the streets of San Juan, not floating above them. For fans, the deepest connection isn't watching an announcement drop on Spotify; it's the feeling that the artist is physically present in their city and that paying attention to their own surroundings can bring them closer to him. Fandom becomes strongest when it works like a treasure hunt in the fan's own neighbourhood.

## Mechanic
Launched in early 2025 by Rimas Music and DDB Latina Puerto Rico (with Bad Bunny himself as Co-Creative Director), the campaign turned San Juan's outdoor media — posters, billboards, transit ads — into live tracking points for the artist's tour, releases, and pop-up events. Each placement carried GPS-style visuals: coordinates, dynamic timestamps, and cryptic messaging that fans could decode to anticipate his next move. The city became a live, unfolding narrative — a city-wide treasure map where following Bad Bunny meant physically paying attention to the streets he represents. Each poster doubled as a social media moment, so the physical layer continuously refuelled organic digital reach. Partners included Google and Spotify.

## Why it worked
P01 (Format as Idea): the idea is the reinvention of the medium itself — static OOH transformed into a live tracking system, so the format carries the message that the artist is here, now, moving among you. P05 (Cultural Hijack): rather than importing a global music-marketing playbook, it ran on Puerto Rican street culture and Bad Bunny's identity as a local, converting fan pride in place into the campaign's operating system. The mechanics manufactured discovery, urgency and personal connection: decoding a poster feels earned in a way an ad impression never does, and every decoded clue was inherently shareable. It set a new standard for music marketing in Latin America — locally rooted enough to feel intimate, resonant enough to win global acclaim.

## Steal
When your talent or brand has genuine local roots, make geography the medium: turn the audience's own streets into the interface. Cryptic beats explicit — coordinates and timestamps invite decoding, and decoding creates investment. And design every physical placement as a photo-first social object, so the OOH buy functions as a content studio.
