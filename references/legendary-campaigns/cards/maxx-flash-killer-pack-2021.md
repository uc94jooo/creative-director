# The Killer Pack (Maxx Flash · 2021)

## Insight
India was facing its biggest outbreak of dengue and malaria — with an overburdened pandemic-era medical system — and the culprit wasn't inside homes, where people already burn repellent coils. It was outside: India's broken garbage collection network creates optimal mosquito breeding grounds, because larvae need just 1 cm of standing water to hatch. Repellents kill mosquitoes indoors while their packaging, discarded at the dump, sits beside the next generation breeding. The category was fighting the symptom and literally throwing away the chance to fight the cause.

## Mechanic
Maxx Flash, a small chemical-free repellent brand, turned its own discarded packaging into a larvicide. The Killer Pack is 100% biodegradable packaging lined and printed with 5% active probiotic Bacillus thuringiensis — a WHO/EPA/ICMR-approved strain that kills mosquito larvae but is harmless to birds, dogs, and other species. Thrown into garbage dumps, dustbins, ponds, or storm drains, the pack disintegrates and treats the water. Coil fights inside; pack fights outside; the vicious cycle breaks. Launched at $0.98 per pack through Public Health Centres in India's disease hot pockets (guided by NCVBDC data), then sold through existing retail and e-commerce. Test communities recorded up to 82% reduction in malaria-carrying Anopheles larvae and up to 68% in dengue-carrying Aedes larvae.

## Why it worked
P17 (Design as Idea): the entire campaign is a packaging decision — the design does the disease control, no media required. P14 (Product as Proof): a challenger brand claiming "protection" proves it by making even its waste protective, extending the product's job beyond the product. It hijacks an existing consumer habit (discarding the pack) rather than asking for a new behavior, and its distribution logic (health centres in outbreak zones) made the public sector the launch media.

## Steal
Your packaging is the one media placement that travels to places your advertising never reaches — including the waste stream. Ask what job the product's discarded shell could do there, and whether the category's biggest externality could be re-engineered into its proof of purpose.
