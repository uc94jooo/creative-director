# ADLaM: An Alphabet To Preserve A Culture (Microsoft · 2023)

## Insight
The Fulani of West Africa — the world's largest nomadic group, 40 million people — spoke Pulaar for generations without an alphabet of their own, forced to cobble their language together from their colonizers' scripts while illiteracy grew and traditions vanished. Two brothers, Ibrahima and Abdoulaye Barry, invented a handwritten alphabet for Pulaar — ADLaM — by closing their eyes and drawing shapes from the sounds of their language. But a handwritten alphabet is useless in the digital world: without encoding, a culture moving online gets shut out of it.

## Mechanic
Microsoft partnered with the Barry brothers to rebuild ADLaM for the digital age. After initial Windows encoding, community feedback showed the letterforms needed major revision — so typeface experts and Fulani graphic-culture specialists redesigned them, compressing centuries of natural letterform evolution into one cycle of real-time community iteration. The final forms drew on Fulani visual culture: hundreds of traditional textile patterns were researched, with the "Kore Totte" (inverted calabash) motif woven into the characters so the community saw themselves in the design. Released as the ADLaM Display typeface across Microsoft 365 on over one billion devices, plus printed and digital learning materials for Guinean schools. Mali moved to recognize ADLaM constitutionally, the first ADLaM dictionary was co-created via #ADLaMRe, and the alphabet will extend to preserve Bambara, Bozo, and Dogon.

## Why it worked
P17 (Design as Idea): the deliverable is the idea — a typeface is simultaneously the campaign, the product, and the cultural infrastructure. P08 (Craft as Message): grounding letterforms in Fulani textile motifs is what converted a Microsoft artifact into Fulani property; the craft research is the respect. P04 (Brand as Activist): Microsoft used its unique asset — OS-level distribution to a billion devices — to do something no NGO could: make a marginalized language a default, not a plug-in. The results compound forever: schools, dictionaries, constitutions.

## Steal
For a tech brand, the deepest purpose play is admission into your infrastructure: whom does your platform silently exclude, and what would shipping their identity as a default look like? And when designing for a community, source the aesthetics from their own material culture — ownership comes from recognition, not consultation.
