# The Lion's Share (Mars · 2018)

## Insight
Animals appear in roughly 20% of all global advertisements — $118 billion is spent every year on advertising that features them — yet the animals themselves get nothing back. While animals thrive on screen, 60% of the world's animal population has been wiped out since 1970. Advertising had been extracting value from the animal kingdom for a century without paying royalties.

## Mechanic
The Lion's Share is an initiative asking brands that use animal imagery in their marketing to donate 0.5% of that media spend to the Lion's Share fund, administered with the United Nations Development Programme. Mars signed first to unlock the private sector; Nielsen validated the 0.5% "size of prize"; Sir David Attenborough launched it globally in June 2018 across 100+ countries. Recruitment targeted Fortune 100 C-suites at the UN General Assembly, Davos, and the One Planet Summit, with donated media from The Economist and Deutsche Welle. Fifty brands signed (JCDecaux, Kering, The Economist), raising $16M — funding 260,000 hectares of Sumatran rainforest, anti-poaching technology in Mozambique, jaguar protection across 13 countries, and coral reef restoration in the West Pacific.

## Why it worked
P01 (Format as Idea): the mechanic lives inside the media budget itself — a levy on animal imagery — so the idea scales automatically with the industry it critiques. P04 (Brand as Activist): Mars didn't run a campaign about wildlife; it re-engineered an industry norm, using its advertiser weight to corral competitors and partners into a standing institution. The 0.5% figure is the genius: small enough that no CMO can refuse, large enough to matter at industry scale — a structural solution, not a donation drive.

## Steal
Find the value your category quietly extracts from the world (imagery, culture, data, natural resources) and design a micro-royalty that pays it back. An ask small enough to be frictionless, attached to a spend that already exists, beats a big one-off charitable gesture — and turns every future media plan into recurring funding.
