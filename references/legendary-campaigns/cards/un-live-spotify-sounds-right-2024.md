# Sounds Right (UN Live / Spotify · 2024)

## Insight
Nature has been working 4.6 billion years for free. Ocean waves, birdsong, rain and wind have contributed to music forever — sampled, remixed, monetized — while wildlife populations fell 69% in 50 years and 1.2M+ species face extinction. Meanwhile the music industry already runs a perfectly good machine for paying creators: royalties. If nature were credited as an artist, the extractive relationship flips — and millions of fans could fund conservation by doing exactly what they already love doing: listening.

## Mechanic
The Museum for the United Nations (UN Live) launched Sounds Right ahead of Earth Day 2024: "NATURE" became an official artist with her own verified profile on major streaming platforms. Global artists — David Bowie x Brian Eno, Ellie Goulding, AURORA, UMI with V of BTS, London Grammar, Bomba Estéreo, Anuv Jain and more — released new tracks or remixed hits "Feat. NATURE," weaving in ecosystem recordings from Colombian rainforests to Indian rains and Borneo. Ambient nature-sound recordings (The Listening Planet, VozTerra) joined the catalog. NATURE's royalties flow through charity EarthPercent to biodiversity conservation in threatened ecosystems, allocated by an expert panel of biologists, Indigenous representatives and conservation funders. Launch ran with Spotify and a global billboard network announcing nature's debut as an artist; the "Feat. NATURE" playlist aggregates the tracks.

## Why it worked
P01 (Format as Idea): the idea is a new business model, not a campaign — a fully functioning, self-renewing royalty stream where every future "Feat. NATURE" release grows the fund, built on infrastructure (streaming credits and splits) that required no new behavior from fans. P04 (Brand as Activist): the UN institution acted instead of advocating, and the personification of "NATURE" as an up-and-coming artist gave newsrooms and fans a character to root for ("it's about time artists and Mother Earth shook hands"). Results: 3.7B+ media impressions, 150M+ streams from 14M+ listeners, NATURE reaching top-1% of artists worldwide, with partners projecting $40M+ for conservation from 600M listeners in four years.

## Steal
Find the industry's existing money plumbing — royalties, residuals, affiliate fees, transaction rails — and add one new payee rather than asking for donations. Personifying the cause as a participant in the system (an artist, a vendor, an author) converts charity into credit: people contribute by consuming what they already enjoy, and the mechanism compounds with every new release instead of ending with the campaign.
