# The Square Meter (HORNBACH · 2023)

## Insight
HORNBACH built its brand on ambitious, joyful, oversized DIY projects. But the European cost-of-living crisis broke the premise: urban dwellers — the biggest and fastest-growing target group — pay more and more money for less and less space. If big projects need big homes, HORNBACH becomes irrelevant to exactly the people it needs most. The campaign had to prove the HORNBACH ethos — creativity, skills, mischief — survives inside a single square meter.

## Mechanic
A brand film that celebrates what a determined DIYer can do with almost no space at all, turning the tightness of urban living from a barrier into the creative brief itself. Production went to unusual lengths for physical precision: instead of actors, the casting agency recruited pantomimes and dancers from Paris's performance community, because the film demanded exact control of body movement to sell life lived in one square meter. Results in Germany: over one billion impressions, preference +5% (making HORNBACH number one in its segment), brand recall +8% to an all-time high, brand perception +10% versus 2022.

## Why it worked
P06 (Limitation as Power): the campaign doesn't apologize for shrinking homes — it weaponizes them, making the smallest possible canvas the proof of the biggest brand belief. That inversion keeps the brand's maximalist spirit intact while flipping its setting to match the audience's reality. P08 (Craft as Message): casting movement artists instead of actors is the idea expressed through production discipline — the body-control virtuosity on screen *is* the argument that skill beats square footage. The film re-earned relevance not by changing what HORNBACH stands for but by relocating it into a one-square-meter world.

## Steal
When a macro trend (shrinking budgets, shrinking spaces, shrinking attention) threatens your brand's core promise, don't dilute the promise — miniaturize the stage and keep the ambition full-size. The constraint your audience suffers from is the most credible place to demonstrate your ethos.
