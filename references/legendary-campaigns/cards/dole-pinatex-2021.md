# Piñatex (Dole Sunshine Company x Ananas Anam · 2021)

## Insight
Next to oil, leather is one of the world's most polluting production sectors — and most "vegan leather" alternatives are plastic-based and ecologically toxic. Meanwhile, the Philippines grows over 2.5 million tons of pineapple a year, and for every ton of fruit harvested, three tons of leaves are left behind; rotting, they emit methane over 20 times more harmful than CO2. The fashion industry's dirtiest problem and the food industry's biggest waste stream turned out to be each other's answer.

## Mechanic
Dole, one of the world's largest pineapple producers, partnered with Ananas Anam to produce Piñatex: a vegan, cruelty-free leather alternative made from the fiber of pineapple leaves that would otherwise rot in the fields. Production is closed-loop — after fiber extraction, residual leaf biomass becomes natural fertilizer — and uses no additional water, land or fertilizer. The supply chain creates rural Filipino jobs in leaf gathering, fiber extraction, logistics and QC, diversifying farmer income. Over 200 brands in 80+ countries adopted the material, including Hugo Boss, H&M and Nike (whose Happy Pineapple sneaker collection is made from Dole leaves), with production scaling 10x and the initiative feeding Dole's zero-waste-by-2025 commitment. Creative Business Transformation Grand Prix, Cannes 2022.

## Why it worked
P14 (Product as Proof): Dole's sustainability promise isn't a pledge — it's a shippable material with better environmental credentials than leather, sold to the world's biggest fashion brands. P17 (Design as Idea): the entire idea lives in one elegant transformation — waste stream in, luxury textile out — instantly understandable and endlessly demonstrable on product. The move also expands the brand's meaning: a fruit company creating change beyond the food industry reframes Dole from producer to circular-economy actor, and every partner brand's sneaker or bag becomes Dole media.

## Steal
Map your production waste and ask which other industry's most polluting input it could replace — the strongest sustainability stories are cross-industry arbitrage, not in-category offsets. Then let adopters carry the story: a material or ingredient branded like Piñatex turns every client product into an advertisement for your transformation.
