# Haaland Payback Time (Supercell · 2024)

## Insight
Erling Haaland is a genuine Clash of Clans player — he started around age ten and had been building his village for over a decade — which made him an authentic celebrity partner. But the sharper insight was about football itself: a striker scoring 200+ goals doesn't just have millions of fans, he has millions of haters — rival supporters who have watched him destroy their teams and want revenge. Every other brand buys a celebrity's fanbase; nobody had ever monetized a celebrity's haters.

## Mechanic
In 2024 Haaland became the first real person featured in Clash of Clans, delighting fans. Then came the twist: the Payback Time Challenge, an official in-game event inviting the world to raid and destroy the real village Haaland had spent a decade building, and steal his resources. The campaign fanned the rivalry everywhere football hate lives: billboards in the stadiums and cities with the most Haaland haters, print ads reacting live to his latest on-pitch performances, an ad stealing the show at rival Manchester United's documentary premiere, and endorsements from the very players he'd humiliated — including the goalkeeper who conceded nine goals to him in one match, and the legends whose records he keeps breaking. Results: 7.9 billion impressions, 34 million players joining the challenge, a 150% increase in new players and 6+ million installs in a single week — the game's most successful campaign in years.

## Why it worked
P03 (Behavior Inversion): the standard celebrity playbook is inverted twice — the brand weaponizes hate instead of admiration, and the star's role is to be attacked rather than adored, which only works because Haaland's fandom of the game is real enough to survive the joke. P02 (Enemy or Conflict): the campaign doesn't manufacture a conflict, it imports football's oldest one — tribal rivalry — and gives it a playable outlet. P05 (Cultural Hijack): live-reacting ads and stadium placements let the campaign surf every matchday news cycle for free. The genius is the conversion mechanic: to get revenge on Haaland you must download and play the game, so the deeper the hate, the stronger the acquisition funnel.

## Steal
When you buy a celebrity, ask what their detractors would pay to do to them — negative emotion is an untapped media channel with higher intent than admiration. Then make the revenge fantasy only executable inside your product, so hostility converts directly into trial.
